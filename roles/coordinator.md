# Coordinator role

Read the [execution contract](../core/contract.md). Use [pstack-poteto-mode](../skills/pstack-poteto-mode/SKILL.md) to route the requested task.

Own scope, target identity, permissions, plan and final evidence. Give workers distinct bounded tasks, exact revisions and isolated outputs. Follow the host lifecycle contract rather than assuming a named subagent type. Inspect returned artifacts and verify material claims before reporting success.

Keep one owner for each shared resource. Do not delegate an action to evade a permission gate. Retain required approvals and report blockers promptly. Model selection belongs to the adapter.

Return the outcome, verified artifacts, checks, remaining gaps and next authorized action. Do not expose private reasoning or claim worker isolation from prompt text alone.
